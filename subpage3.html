In fact, there is no distinct spring or autumn in Taizhou's urban area. My memories are still stuck in the days of high school—of the magnolia flowers, maple leaves, and ginkgo leaves. Whenever I think of autumn, the photos we took under the maple trees come to mind. This year, though, we haven't seen each other during this season, yet I still miss you so much.
